# 🛒 Prozone Super Market KPI Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Prozone Super Market KPI Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project created using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze supermarket sales performance, profitability, customer orders, product pricing, and return rates.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of essential **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**, helping businesses monitor financial performance, evaluate operational efficiency, and make data-driven decisions.

This project demonstrates my ability to transform business data into meaningful insights through data visualization, KPI calculations, and analytical reporting.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- 📈 Monitor overall supermarket revenue and sales performance.
- 💰 Evaluate gross profit and profitability.
- 🛍️ Track customer orders and units sold.
- 💵 Analyze average unit prices and order values.
- 🔄 Measure product return rates.
- 📊 Present important business metrics in a visually appealing dashboard.
- 🎯 Support data-driven business decisions.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard displays eight essential business metrics.

| 📊 KPI | 📈 Value | 📝 Description |
|---|---|---|
| 💰 Total Revenue | 14.01K | Total revenue generated from supermarket sales |
| 🛒 Total Orders | 30 | Total number of customer orders |
| 💵 Average Unit Price | 57.69 | Average selling price per unit |
| 🧾 Average Order Value | 466.85 | Average revenue generated per order |
| 📈 Gross Profit | 7.95K | Gross profit generated from sales |
| 🎯 Profit Margin | 56.76% | Percentage of revenue retained as gross profit |
| 🔄 Return Rate | 20.00% | Percentage of returns based on the dashboard's return-rate calculation |
| 📦 Total Units Sold | 252 | Total quantity of products sold |

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 💰 1. Revenue Performance

- Total revenue reached **14.01K**.
- The supermarket processed **30 customer orders**.
- Average order value was **466.85**, indicating the average revenue generated per transaction.

### 📈 2. Profitability Analysis

- Gross profit reached approximately **7.95K**.
- The supermarket achieved a **56.76% gross profit margin**.
- This indicates that approximately 56.76% of revenue remained after the costs included in the gross profit calculation.

### 📦 3. Sales Volume Analysis

- A total of **252 units** were sold.
- Average unit price was **57.69**.
- Tracking sales volume alongside revenue helps businesses understand product movement and pricing performance.

### 🔄 4. Return Rate Analysis

- The dashboard reports a **20.00% return rate**.
- Returns are an important operational metric because they can affect profitability, inventory management, and customer satisfaction.
- Further investigation could help identify which products or categories contribute most to returns.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard's displayed metrics, the following actions could help improve supermarket performance:

**1. 🔄 Investigate Product Returns**

Analyze returned products, return reasons, and product categories to identify opportunities to reduce the 20% return rate.

**2. 📈 Improve Sales Performance**

Examine high-performing products and purchasing patterns to identify opportunities to increase revenue and order volume.

**3. 💰 Maintain Profitability**

Monitor gross profit margins and evaluate pricing and cost-management strategies.

**4. 🛒 Increase Average Order Value**

Explore product bundles, cross-selling opportunities, and targeted promotions to encourage larger purchases.

**5. 📦 Optimize Inventory Management**

Use sales quantity and product demand analysis to support inventory planning and reduce excess stock.
